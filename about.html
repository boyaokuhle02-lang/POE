About Coastal Cut - East London

Our Story in East London

Coastal Cut Barbershop opened in 2022 at 45 Beachfront. East London. We bring premium grooming with coastal vibes. Our mission: Everyone deserves a fresh cut, so we run Sponsor a Cut for local students.

Our Barbers - Master Barbers

Our master barbers have 10+ years of experiences in skin faddes, beard sculpting, classic cuts. Trained in Cape Town and Johannesburg, now serving East London.

Community Values - Sponser a Cut

Every month we sponser 20 FREE haircuts for HIGH SCHOOL LEARNERS in East London. Your sponsorship of R150 helps a learner get a fresh cut for school interviews.
